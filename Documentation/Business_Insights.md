# 📊 Financial Performance – Business Insights & Recommendations

## 🚀 Project Overview

This analysis evaluates financial performance across sales, profitability, customer segments, products, countries and discount categories.

The objective is to identify important financial patterns and generate actionable business recommendations.

## 💰 1. Overall Financial Performance

The dashboard highlights the following financial KPIs:

- Total Sales: 118.73M
- Total Gross Sales: 127.93M
- Total Profit: 16.89M
- Total Discount: 9.21M
- Total COGS: 101.83M
- Total Units Sold: 1.13M
- Profit Margin: 14.23%

**Business Insight:**

The dashboard provides a consolidated view of revenue, expenses, discounts and profitability, helping evaluate overall financial performance.

## 👥 2. Profit Analysis by Segment

The dashboard compares profitability across five customer segments:

- Government
- Small Business
- Channel Partners
- Midmarket
- Enterprise

Key observations:

- Government contributes approximately 11.39M in profit (65.04%).
- Small Business contributes approximately 4.14M (23.66%).
- Channel Partners contributes approximately 1.32M (7.52%).

**Business Insight:**

Government and Small Business together account for the majority of the displayed profit.

Understanding these segments can help businesses identify their major profit contributors.

## 🌍 3. Sales Performance by Country

The dashboard compares sales and profit across five countries:

- United States
- Canada
- France
- Germany
- Mexico

Key observations:

- United States records approximately 25M in sales.
- Canada records approximately 25M in sales.
- France records approximately 24M in sales.
- Germany records approximately 24M in sales.
- Mexico records approximately 21M in sales.

**Business Insight:**

Sales performance is relatively close across the first four markets, while Mexico records comparatively lower sales.

Country-level analysis helps identify geographical performance differences.

## 🏷️ 4. Sales by Discount Band

The dashboard evaluates sales across four discount categories:

- Medium: Approximately 39M
- High: Approximately 37M
- Low: Approximately 35M
- None: Approximately 8M

**Business Insight:**

The Medium discount band records the highest displayed sales.

Businesses should evaluate discount strategies alongside profitability to understand whether higher sales translate into better financial returns.

## 📦 5. Profit by Product

The dashboard compares profitability across six products:

- Paseo: 4.8M
- VTT: 3.0M
- Amarilla: 2.8M
- Velo: 2.3M
- Montana: 2.1M
- Carretera: 1.8M

**Business Insight:**

Paseo is the highest-profit product among those displayed, while Carretera records the lowest.

Product-level profitability analysis helps businesses identify stronger and weaker product contributors.

## 📅 6. Sales Trend Analysis

The dashboard presents monthly sales and gross sales trends.

Key observations:

- Sales fluctuate across reporting periods.
- Gross sales and total sales follow similar patterns.
- Certain months demonstrate higher revenue than others.

**Business Insight:**

Monitoring monthly performance can help identify revenue fluctuations and support financial planning.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

### 1. Improve Profitability
Monitor profit margins regularly and identify opportunities to improve operational efficiency.

### 2. Focus on Major Customer Segments
Understand the profitability drivers of Government and Small Business segments.

### 3. Optimize Discount Strategies
Evaluate the relationship between discount levels, revenue and profit before changing pricing strategies.

### 4. Strengthen Product Performance
Monitor high-profit products and investigate opportunities to improve lower-performing products.

### 5. Evaluate Geographical Markets
Compare country-level sales and profitability to identify potential market opportunities.

### 6. Monitor Financial Trends
Use monthly financial reporting to identify changes in sales and profitability.

## 🎯 Conclusion

The Financial Performance Analytics Dashboard provides an interactive overview of business financial performance.

Through analysis of sales, profit, discounts, customer segments, products and geographical markets, the project demonstrates how financial data can be transformed into meaningful business insights.

The findings can support better financial monitoring, pricing decisions, product evaluation and business planning.
